KOLINN_NAV

A terminal-inspired mega menu built on the KOLINN Design System: Nord tokens, Fira typefaces, an 8px rhythm, and co-equal dark & light themes.

---

by KOLINN

Architecture & design philosophy

01 Performance

Zero dependencies. State is class-driven with exactly one layout read per interaction, a ResizeObserver keeps the flyout height truthful, and item animations stick to transform and opacity for a consistent 60fps.

02 Accessibility

Nord pairs re-tested for AA, inert-based focus containment, honest ARIA state (aria-expanded / aria-controls), :focus-visible detection, Escape with focus restoration, a skip link, and full prefers-reduced-motion respect.

03 Design engineering

Token-first: Nord primitives resolve through semantic roles, Fira Code prompts and Fira Sans prose sit on an 8px rhythm, and a frosted full-screen curtain spotlights the open flyout.
